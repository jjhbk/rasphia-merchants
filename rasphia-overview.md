## The one-minute version

Your customers are changing how they find things. Instead of typing keywords into a search bar and scrolling through ten links, they're asking AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT — in full sentences, with real detail:

> *"My mom's visiting this weekend — I need a salon that's genuinely good with thick, greying curly hair, gentle with sensitive scalps, somewhere calm. ₹2,000 for both of us, Saturday morning, near Banjara Hills."*
>
> *"Find me a yoga studio that's gentle on a bad knee, with morning batches and a patient instructor."*
>
> *"I want a handwoven gift with a real story behind it, under ₹3,000, delivered this week."*

The AI answers with **one confident recommendation** — matched on every constraint — not ten links.

Either your business is that answer, or it doesn't exist in that conversation at all.

## Why this is different from every marketing channel you've tried

**These customers arrive pre-sold.** By the time an AI recommends you, the customer has already explained exactly what they want — their budget, their need, their preferences. The AI did the qualifying, the comparing, and the convincing. What reaches you isn't a browser. It's a buyer.

**You can't buy your way in — which is your advantage.** There is no ad slot inside an AI assistant's answer. Recommendations are earned through the quality and depth of your information, not marketing budget. A skilled independent stylist with a rich, well-structured profile can beat a big chain that hasn't shown up in this channel yet. For once, the playing field tilts toward whoever is genuinely good — not whoever spends the most.

**The window is open right now.** AI-assisted local search is growing explosively, yet only a tiny fraction of local businesses ever get recommended. The rest are invisible — not ranked low, simply *absent*. The businesses that structure themselves for this channel early become the ones AI assistants learn to trust and return to. Early movers on Google Maps and reviews owned the last decade of local discovery. This is that moment, again.

## The problem: your business is invisible to AI

Right now, an AI assistant knows almost nothing useful about you. At best: a name, a pin on a map, a star rating. It doesn't know that you specialize in bridal makeup for mature skin. That your Tuesday batches are beginner-friendly. That your carpentry work comes with a two-year guarantee. That your pottery uses clay from your own village.

**All the things that actually make customers choose you are locked in your head, your Instagram captions, and your regulars' word of mouth — in forms no AI can read, reason over, or recommend from.**

So when someone asks the exact question you are the perfect answer to, the AI recommends someone else. Or worse — nobody local at all.
